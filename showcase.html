Before and after learning

Small changes make the message clearer

Original composition → improved crop

A tighter crop can remove distractions and make the subject's relationship with the sky more intentional.

Incorrect → corrected white balance

Choose a white balance that keeps skin and important neutral colours believable, unless a colour cast is intentional.

Distracting → simplified background

Changing angle or depth of field can separate a subject from visual clutter without changing the subject itself.

Flat → intentional colour and motion

A clear colour decision and a deliberate shutter speed can turn ordinary traffic into a photograph with energy.

The comparisons explain techniques; they are not literal before-and-after edits of the same file.
