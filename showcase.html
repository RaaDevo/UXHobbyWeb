Photography Techniques in Practice

Subject separation

Clear Subject

Environmental framing

Intentional Framing

Trees around the edge form a natural frame that contains the view and guides attention deeper into the forest.

Controlled colour

Strong Use of Colour

A limited group of vivid stage colours creates energy while directing the eye toward the brightest area.

Shutter speed

Motion or Stillness

A deliberate exposure turns moving traffic into visible light trails, using blur as part of the story instead of an accident.

Balanced Composition

A focused crop removes unnecessary surroundings so the building's strong lines and carefully spaced shapes carry the frame.
